ADHD-Friendly Printable Planner is a printable PDF of 30 original planning sheets in six sheet families, five layouts each — designed around one principle: few choices per page.

  • 5 × today's 3 things — one big date line, exactly three large numbered priority boxes with checkboxes, and a small "nice to have" area that only unlocks when the three are done. The anti-overwhelm page.
  • 5 × brain dump — full-page free-write sheets (dot grid, wide-ruled, margin-gutter and gentle-prompt variants) with a boxed "park it here" zone at the bottom for everything that can wait. No wrong answers.
  • 5 × time blocks — a visual day in 2-hour blocks (6am–10pm) with a "top 1" star box. Big block labels, no 15-minute granularity — rough shapes are enough.
  • 5 × dopamine menu — a restaurant-clean menu of quick wins: starters (5-minute tasks), mains (15–30 minutes), and a dessert box for your reward. One variant adds a "nothing fits? do just one thing" escape hatch for hard days.
  • 5 × week launch — the whole week on one page: seven big day boxes with ONE priority line and checkbox each, a "one thing that matters this week" banner, and a tiny habit tracker (3 habits × 5 dots — five days counts).
  • 5 × break it down — task-initiation worksheets: one big task box, four step boxes each with a checkbox and a "done feels like:" line, and a press-here start button.

Every sheet is black on white, US Letter (8.5 × 11 in), with generous margins, 11–14pt labels and 24–26pt titles — no tiny text anywhere. Print at 100% scale, use one sheet a day, re-print whenever.

Design language, honest scope: these pages are built for time-blindness — few choices, big type, visual time. They are a design aid, not a medical product: nothing in this pack diagnoses, treats, or fixes anything, and no therapeutic claims are made.

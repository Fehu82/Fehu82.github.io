# House style

The rules the generators enforce. `tools/generate_hooks.py` and
`tools/generate_pins.py` embed these in their system prompts — edit them there
to change behaviour, and mirror the change here so the reasoning stays recorded.

## Never

- Invent a feature, page count, bonus or file format that is not in the product
  description
- Fake scarcity: countdowns, "only N left", "price goes up tonight"
- Fabricated testimonials, review counts or sales figures
- Income claims or get-rich framing of any kind
- Imply the art is hand-drawn — every Fehu Lab product is AI-assisted and
  disclosed as such under Etsy's Creativity Standards

## Always

- Lead with the words a buyer would actually type into a search box
- Say concretely what lands in the buyer's hands: page count, page size, format
- Vary the **buying motive** between variants, not just the wording. Two
  variants aimed at the same motive are one variant.
- Keep the AI disclosure true in every piece of copy

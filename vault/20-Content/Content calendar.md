## Channel notes

**Pinterest** — the primary channel for printable coloring books. It is a search
engine, not a social feed: pin descriptions are indexed, and pins routinely take
4–8 weeks to find traffic. Judge it on quarters, not days. Post consistently,
2–5 pins/week, each linking to the product page with `utm_medium=pinterest`.

**TikTok** — flip-through and time-lapse colouring clips of real pages. Short,
no voice-over needed. Link in bio to the landing page, not to a single product.

**Etsy** — not open yet. Worth considering as a second storefront since the
listings already carry Etsy-compliant AI disclosure. Different economics: fees
and per-listing costs, but buyer intent is far higher than social. Decide
deliberately, do not drift into it.

**Email** — dead until [[Wire email capture]] is done. Once live it is the only
channel that is actually owned; everything else is rented.

## Rules

- Ship real page art. Never a generated image that is not in the product.
- Every off-site link carries its UTM tag or the week's data is worthless.
- One product per pin. Bundle pitches convert worse than a single clear offer.

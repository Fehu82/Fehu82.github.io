Return & refund policy

Every Greyson Prints item is made to order — production starts when you buy, so we cannot accept returns or exchanges for buyer's remorse or wrong-size orders (each product page lists sizes; check before ordering).

Damaged, misprinted, or defective items: contact us within 30 days of delivery with a photo of the issue and we will send a free replacement or issue a full refund — your choice. This mirrors our fulfillment partner Printful's misprint/damage policy.

Lost in transit: claim within 30 days of the estimated delivery date and we will reship or refund after carrier confirmation.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method via Stripe.
